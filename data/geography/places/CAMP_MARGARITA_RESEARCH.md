## Published location context — 2026-09-08

The map record now contains a short editorial paragraph and its web references.
DVIDS photo 3127806, supplied by Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton, identifies a
5th Marines rifle-company office at Margarita in 1964:
https://www.dvidshub.net/image/3127806/camp-margarita-1964

The title/caption date is 1964; the catalog's Date Taken field says 2017 and its
VIRIN begins 420925. Preserve this metadata discrepancy: 1964 is the caption's
attribution, not a independently verified exposure date. HTML caption reviewed;
no image or PDF inspection. Candidate period-context photo, not identified as
2/5 or Hotel. DVIDS labels it public domain subject to its copyright policy;
no image copied or published. The paragraph's administrative-life phrase is
editorial interpretation of the office caption. Battalion residence and
training context derive from the existing July–December chronology, not this
photograph. No specific barracks or camp boundary inferred.
