# Chapter 01 major cities

Adds Honolulu and Naha to the California city markers for the Pacific journey. Approximate city reference positions are stored in CHAPTER_01_CITIES.json with source URLs. Orange dots and black labels share the Major Cities toggle. These are reference points, not claims that the battalion entered either city.

Wide views show Hawaii (regional title near Oahu) and Okinawa. Editorial label positions are [23, -158] and [28, 127.95]; they are not boundary centroids. Between map zoom 5.5 and 8, these titles fade out as Honolulu and Naha dots and labels fade in. The Major Cities toggle controls both levels.
