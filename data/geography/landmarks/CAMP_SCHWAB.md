# Camp Schwab

- Stable ID: `camp-schwab`
- Type: Marine Corps base; Okinawa, Japan.
- [Modern reference geometry](../places/CAMP_SCHWAB.md): OpenStreetMap relation
  5973045, retrieved 2026-09-07, ODbL attribution retained on the map.
- Geometry status: coastal-base polygon, 21 joined outer ways and one closed
  ring of 213 vertices. Does not include the separately mapped Central Training
  Area or Henoko Ammunition Supply Point. Not a verified 1966 perimeter.
- Label position: editorial placement within the mapped area, not a barracks
  coordinate or historical evidence.
- Associated map areas: [Central Training Area](../places/CENTRAL_TRAINING_AREA.md)
  and [Henoko Ammunition Supply Point](../places/HENOKO_AMMUNITION.md).
  Displayed in yellow-green alongside the Marine-green coastal base, retaining
  separate identities. This grouping is navigation, not a historical ownership
  claim. Central Training Area is not Northern Training Area.
- Unit context: [1966 road chapter evidence](../../units/5th_marines/2nd_battalion/research/1966_ROAD_EVIDENCE.md)
  and chronology 1201048067 support the Camp Schwab training narrative.
- Future album: period context and primary unit photos must retain separate
  source, rights, date, and identity records. No album is implemented.
- Historical boundary research lead: Okinawa Prefecture Camp Schwab environmental
  atlas, https://www.pref.okinawa.lg.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/004/872/karte_007.pdf.
  Not inspected or used to trace this geometry; its historical applicability is unverified.

## Map display hierarchy

Central Training Area and Henoko Ammunition Supply Point are display sub-areas of Camp Schwab. Both outlines and labels hide when the Camp Schwab boundary enters its under-20px compact state, and return on zoom-in. This grouping does not assert historical administrative ownership.

Central Training Area now uses [one combined display outline](../places/CENTRAL_TRAINING_AREA_DISPLAY.md), joining the original sections across narrow gaps. Original geometry remains separately preserved.
