## Display geometry

The map now uses [the modern complex geometry](../places/PEARL_HARBOR.md): joint-base land including Hickam, plus harbor water. The original point above remains an orientation reference. Full outlines appear on zoom-in and a single callout at overview scale. This is not a verified 1966 perimeter.
