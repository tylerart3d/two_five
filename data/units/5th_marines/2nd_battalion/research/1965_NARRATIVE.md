## Research limits

The regimental history describes the replacement battalion receiving the 3/9 designation on 18 July; the local chronology records the returning battalion becoming 2/5 on 19 July. These are retained as separate recorded statements; the narrative does not force them into a single date. The 1965 official-history passage describes the outward-bound exchange in late June. Exact administrative timing requires reconciliation.

The immediate route is Vietnam–Okinawa–United States; McPartlin's documented Korean combat service does not establish a battalion return from Korea. His biography uses LB65-002 (printed p. 10), the existing landing account (pp. 10–11), and chronology 1201048065, p. 1 for command at redesignation. “First commander” applies only to this redesignated battalion, not all of 2/5 history.
