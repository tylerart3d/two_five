## Completion criterion

For each source, retain a coverage ledger mapping every relevant passage to an
event, a supporting assertion/context record, a duplicate, or an unresolved
review item. Only mark a source complete after that audit. The current keyword
candidate index is not such a ledger and is not a full-book completeness claim.
New extraction must use the resolved evidence layers and respect human fixes;
this catalog build consumes existing editorial records and does not implement
that resolver or re-extract source OCR.
