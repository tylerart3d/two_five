## LP-01 — Charles E. McPartlin Jr., Hill 327

- **Usage:** predecessor-unit leadership photograph; depicts 3/9 command context,
  not a 2/5 or Hotel photograph. Existing [1965 history research](LANDING_BUILDUP_1965.md)
  and the chronology establish the name/command bridge into the tree.
- **Record:** [Commons full-exposure record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hill327DaNang1965.jpg).
- **Image:** [884 × 857 JPEG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/57/Hill327DaNang1965.jpg).
  This is a reproduction, not the archival negative.
- **Originating publication:** [USMC, The Landing and the Buildup, 1965](https://www.marines.mil/portals/1/Publications/U.S.%20Marines%20in%20Vietnam_The%20Landing%20and%20the%20Buildup%201965%20%20PCN%2019000307600.pdf?ver=2017-04-19-164500-117), printed p. 18
  (page confirmed by existing extraction/index; PDF page index not verified here).
- **Caption evidence (paraphrase):** officers look across terrain from Hill 327,
  west of Da Nang. The left-to-right list places McPartlin second, between
  Krulak and Karch, with Muir at the other end. It gives McPartlin's full name,
  lieutenant-colonel rank, and 3d Battalion, 9th Marines command.
- **Date:** Commons says circa 1965 and categorizes the image under March 1965.
  Exact exposure date remains unverified; do not derive a day from nearby events.
- **Credit:** USMC. Official-publication search text reads `USMC Photo A183859`;
  Commons author field instead contains the corrupted string `[USMC Photo A 1838^9]`.
  Verify the identifier on the scan before treating A183859 as an archival lookup key.
- **Rights evidence:** Commons explicitly tags the image public domain as an
  official-duty USMC work. Preserve USMC credit; this is the file page's rights
  assessment, not clearance of every item in the source book.
- **Caution:** [Joseph E. Muir.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Joseph_E._Muir.jpg)
  repeats the group caption but is a narrow derivative named for Muir. Do not
  select it for McPartlin merely because the description mentions him. Use the
  full exposure, confirm second-from-left, and keep any future crop linked to it.

## LP-02 — Littleton W. T. Waller II, later-life photograph

- **Source and image container:** [Fortitudine, vol. 25, no. 2, Fall 1995](https://www.usmcu.edu/Portals/218/Fortitudine%20Vol%2025%20No%202.pdf), printed p. 9.
  No separate image URL discovered. PDF page index and image region unverified.
- **Caption evidence (paraphrase):** the retired lieutenant colonel stands on
  the left with family and Edwin H. Simmons, examining a restored portrait at
  Quantico's Tri-modular Club. The caption explicitly names Littleton W. T.
  Waller II and distinguishes him from the two major generals of his family.
- **Date/context:** published Fall 1995; event/exposure date not supplied by
  the retrieved caption. This is a later-life image, not Vietnam or Pendleton 1965.
- **Identity:** unusual full name, suffix and Marine officer status make this a
  strong lead. The official [1966 history index](https://www.marines.mil/Portals/1/Publications/US%20Marines%20in%20Vietnam%20An%20Expanding%20War%201966%20%20PCN%2019000308600_6.pdf)
  lists Maj Littleton W. T. Waller II separately from MajGen Littleton W. T.
  Waller (printed p. 389). Complete the career bridge before publishing a crop.
- **Photographer / photo number / rights:** not established from indexed text;
  official hosting alone is insufficient to assign an image-specific license.
- **Avoid:** the restored painted portrait depicts the earlier general, not
  the 1965 major. Also reject Tyrone Waller II and Littleton Waller Tazewell II
  search results; these are different full names.

## LP-03 — Jerome “Jerry” John Doherty obituary portrait lead

- **Record:** [Allnutt Funeral Service / Dignity Memorial obituary](https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/loveland-co/jerome-doherty-12073639).
  The indexed page identifies an obituary image for Jerome “Jerry” John Doherty;
  the underlying portrait has not been visually checked here.
- **Identity evidence:** obituary describes a Marine captain with a Silver Star
  for Vietnam service. [Hall of Valor recipient 40074](https://valor.militarytimes.com/recipient/recipient-40074/)
  gives Jerome John Doherty Jr., service number 0-88119, Company H, 2d Battalion,
  5th Marines, and the 26 January 1967 action. This supports a probable match,
  but the obituary does not itself supply the unit or service number.
- **Date/context:** obituary dates are 28 September 1941–9 November 2024;
  neither is the photo's exposure date. Photo date and location unknown.
- **Image URL:** unresolved. Text browsing succeeded; direct HTML metadata
  retrieval was blocked. No bypass attempted and no guessed CDN link recorded.
- **Credit / rights:** photographer, contributor and reuse permission unknown.
  Public-display status pending identity, image and permission review.
- **Other useful record:** [Hotel association award page](https://hotel25vv.com/silver-star-jerome-doherty)
  reproduces the award identity, but its inspected HTML images are a medal and
  site graphics, not a Doherty portrait. The association's generated historical
  pages are discovery aids, not independent corroboration of this project's facts.
