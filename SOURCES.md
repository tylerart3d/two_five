# Research sources and attribution

The primary archival source for this project is **The Vietnam Center and Sam
Johnson Vietnam Archive at Texas Tech University**.

- [Virtual Vietnam Archive and its copyright notice](https://www.vietnam.ttu.edu/virtualarchive/)
- [Search original archival records](https://vva.vietnam.ttu.edu/)

This repository's code ownership notice does not relicense archival material
or claim ownership of historical facts. Source materials retain their
applicable rights and restrictions. Attribution is not a grant of permission
to redistribute a source document. Follow the archive's notice and any
item-specific rights information when publishing excerpts or reproductions.

## Required source trail

Every published extraction must resolve to an original source, not merely the
archive homepage. Preserve:

- Archive identifier, document title, issuing unit, and covered dates.
- Verified Texas Tech item-record URL and original PDF URL where available.
- PDF page index and printed page label where those differ.
- The supporting passage and its location within the page/OCR transcription.
- Extraction method, model/version when applicable, and processing date.
- Review status, uncertainty, and a history of corrections.

Names, dates, grid references, movements, casualties, and operations need
traceable assertions. A normalized person or place may have multiple source
assertions; retain them rather than overwriting conflicting evidence.

Narrative claims must link to supporting assertions and source pages. Separate
documented statements, editorial interpretation, and attributed recollections.
Do not treat model output as an independent historical source.

## Publication policy

Use verified links to Texas Tech originals as the default for PDFs. This
repository now contains local research evidence and Markdown catalogs under
data/. Original assets in _files/ directories are Git-ignored. The local proof
serves one explicitly allowlisted source PDF; no PDFs are included in the public
build. Add reviewed public data through a deliberate publication
workflow; local import is not publication or historical verification.

For future non-Texas-Tech sources, cite the actual originating source and record
its rights; never attribute those materials to Texas Tech. Check publication
permission before including privately supplied recollections or materials.

Preserve raw OCR unchanged. Keep corrections in a separate, reproducible layer.
Missing records, uncertain coordinates, and unresolved identities must remain
visible rather than being filled with invented certainty.
