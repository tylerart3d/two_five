# Operation TENDERTOUCH: research leads

Saved September 13, 2026 at Brent's request. Editorial research bookmarks;
not new event data or evidence ingested into the website.

## Current findings

- **Camp Pendleton confirmed:** 2/5's July–December 1965 command chronology,
  Texas Tech item 1201048065, PDF page 3 (printed C-14-3), explicitly places
  the exercise at Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton, California.
- The reviewed entry records November 3–5 participation by reinforced 1st
  Platoon, Golf Company, under 2ndLt W. W. Jeffery, as aggressors for the
  Army's 3rd Brigade, 5th Infantry Division (Mechanized), Fort Carson.
- The dates describe the Marine entry; the full Army exercise duration is
  not independently established.
- **Amphibious landing unconfirmed:** no TENDERTOUCH-specific Army order,
  after-action report, or contemporary article establishing one has been
  located in the public web search so far. Absence from these results is
  not evidence that no landing occurred.
- Preserve the human-confirmed spellings TENDERTOUCH and JEFFERY. See
  [human correction](../data/corrections/human/tendertouch-jeffery.json).

## Distinguish the 1964 precedent

The official [Marine Corps chronology, 1947–1964](https://www.marines.mil/Portals/1/Publications/A%20Chronology%20of%20the%20United%20States%20Marine%20Corps%201947-1964%20%20PCN%2019000318200.pdf),
printed page 69, describes BAND WAGON, November 10–12, 1964, involving the
Army's **1st** Brigade, 5th Infantry Division, at Camp Pendleton, with
amphibious training and Marine insurgent forces. It is a different exercise,
year, and brigade. Do not transfer its amphibious description to TENDERTOUCH.
