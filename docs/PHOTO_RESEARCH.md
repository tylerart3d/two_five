# Photo research direction

Initial scope and lead review, September 7, 2026. No new photographs downloaded
or published in this pass. No verified 1965 Hotel/Camp Pendleton photo has yet
been selected for the new application.

## Two uses

**Primary — 2/5, especially Hotel:** photographs with evidence connecting the
subjects or photographer to the battalion at the relevant place and time.
Record who took the photo separately from who appears in it. A photographer's
service in Hotel does not by itself identify every pictured Marine as Hotel.

**Period context:** photographs showing the relevant base, landscape, ship,
equipment, or activity at an appropriate date. Caption them as contextual when
they do not depict the particular unit/event. Do not use a later base layout or
equipment variant as evidence for 1965 merely because it is Vietnam-era.

Keep usage classification separate from archival authenticity: a period-context
photograph can itself be a primary historical source about its actual subject.

## Catalog requirements

Preserve source record and image URLs, original caption, photographer/collection,
date or range and its evidence, location evidence, depicted people/unit evidence,
rights and permission status, intended chapter/use, and identification confidence.
Retain originals separately from crops and display derivatives. Alternate scans
of the same exposure should share an identity rather than count as corroboration.

## Existing research worth retaining

The previous An Hoa photo catalogs already preserve credits, dates, checksums,
source URLs, and reuse status. Their initial README lists 48 records; a separate
veteran-site batch reports 34 saved files. Most material concerns later years.
Those README counts were inspected; image files were not recounted in this pass.
They are useful future assets, not substitutes for verified 1965 imagery.

## Leads and current status

- [Hotel 2/5 Mail Call](https://hotel25vv.com/mail-call): a veteran specifically
  asks about company/platoon photos taken on Okinawa before deployment in 1966.
  This is a targeted lead for locating primary photographs, not an acquired
  collection. No outreach has been sent.
- [2/5 association photo index](https://2ndbn5thmarines.com/pictures/index.html):
  existing local research identifies relevant collections, but this pass could
  not retrieve the index. Dates and individual photo evidence remain to check.
- [Barry Broman photographic essay, Marine Corps University](https://www.usmcu.edu/Outreach/Marine-Corps-University-Press/MCH/Marine-Corps-History-Summer-2020/PHOTOGRAPHIC-ESSAY-With-Hotel-Company-2d-Battalion-5th-Marines-in-An-Hoa/):
  accessible Hotel Company collection at An Hoa. Keep for the later-period
  backlog, not as imagery of 1965 Pendleton.
- [Official USMC 1965 history, PDF segment](https://www.marines.mil/Portals/1/Publications/U.S.%20Marines%20in%20Vietnam_The%20Landing%20and%20the%20Buildup%201965%20%20PCN%2019000307600_6.pdf):
  search text identifies a March 1965 photograph including USS Princeton with
  other ships. Candidate period context only: caption names BLT 2/9 and HMM-365,
  not 2/5. Exact image, page, credit, and rights need inspection before selection.

Prioritize 1965 Camp Margarita/Pendleton, 2/5 or Hotel group photographs,
leadership portraits, and the documented November USS Princeton exercise.
Search equipment only after identifying what the sources establish was used.

Search results from Hotel companies in 2/1, 2/4, 2/7, or 2/9 must not be silently
classified as Hotel 2/5. Earlier project-generated pages are discovery aids,
not independent corroboration of their own narrative claims.
