## First experience

Use the July–December 1965 chronology (archive identifier 1201048065) as an
initial anchor. The locally preserved OCR has three labeled pages and covers
reorganization, return to San Diego, leadership changes, and training.
PDF page alignment and extracted claims still need verification before release.

Working chapter direction: rebuilding and preparing at Camp Pendleton. Let
command assignments appear as an expandable sequence rather than making every
staff change a separate story chapter. Retain every sourced change in detail.
Show Hotel-specific evidence explicitly; battalion activities do not establish
Hotel participation unless the source supports that connection.

Start with Camp Margarita within Camp Pendleton as a source-named location.
Do not infer exact 1965 barracks or training footprints from modern maps. Date
ranges and approximate locations must remain approximate in the interface.

The sparse 1965 period will validate the story/evidence interaction. A dense
1966 sample must subsequently validate timeline scale and hourly navigation.
